# Clinical Study Protocol Synopsis

| Parameter | Protocol Specification |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Study Title** | Deranged Liver Enzymes in COVID-19 Patients and its Impacts on Inpatient Prognosis |
| **Study Design** | Prospective, longitudinal observational registry |
| **Study Site** | Mayo Hospital, King Edward Medical University, Lahore |
| **Study Population** | Adult patients admitted with PCR-confirmed or suspected SARS-CoV-2 infection |
| **Sample Size** | N = 224 evaluable participants |
| **Primary Objective** | To assess the prevalence and trajectory of hepatic enzyme derangement (ALT, AST, ALP, Bilirubin) between Day 1 and Day 7 of admission. |
| **Secondary Objectives** | 1. Evaluate associations between baseline comorbidities (DM, HTN, IHD, CLD) and liver derangement.<br>2. Determine the prognostic association between deranged enzymes and critical outcomes (ICU transfer, in-hospital mortality). |
| **Primary Endpoints** | Paired difference in mean serum ALT and Bilirubin levels between Day 1 and Day 7. |
| **Safety Endpoints** | Incidence of Acute Liver Injury (ALT > 3x ULN), expedited Serious Adverse Events (SAEs), and tertiary clinical disposition (Ward, HDU, ICU, In-hospital Death). |
| **Regulatory Framework** | Declaration of Helsinki, ICH GCP E6(R3), and local institutional ethics review. |
